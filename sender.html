Pilot guide (Sender)

  1. Create a request (pickup, drop‑off, item, suggested price).
  2. Review offers and accept one driver.
  3. Fund escrow when prompted (Stripe test mode during pilot).
  4. After delivery: confirm to release escrow immediately, or it auto‑releases after 24 hours.

DeliveryMate is a facilitator only. We do not provide dispute resolution, refunds, or reversals after release.
