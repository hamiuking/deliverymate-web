Pilot guide (Sender)

  1. Create a request and save the Request ID.
  2. Review offers and accept one approved driver.
  3. Fund escrow when prompted (Stripe test mode in pilot).
  4. After delivery: confirm to release escrow immediately, or auto-release after 24 hours.

DeliveryMate is a facilitator only. We do not provide dispute resolution, refunds, or reversals after escrow release.
