How it works (Sender)

  1. Create a request — Describe what you need delivered.
  2. Review offers — Approved drivers submit their price.
  3. Accept & pay — Choose a driver and fund escrow (Stripe).
  4. Track delivery — Your driver updates you at pickup and delivery.
  5. Confirm delivery — Release payment immediately, or it auto-releases after 24 hours.
