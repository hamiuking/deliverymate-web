Pilot guide (Sender)

  • Create a request (pickup, drop-off, item, suggested price).
  • Receive offers from approved drivers.
  • Accept an offer and fund escrow (Stripe test mode).
  • After delivery: confirm to release immediately, or auto-release after 24 hours.
DeliveryMate is a facilitator only — not a courier, employer, or agent. No disputes, no refunds, no reversals.
