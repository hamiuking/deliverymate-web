DeliveryMate is running a controlled pilot. Register first, then create a request (sender) or make an offer (driver). Driver applications require manual approval during the pilot.

Invite-only pilot. Request access via admin@deliverymate.nz.

How it works (pilot)

Invite-only
  • DeliveryMate is a facilitator only. Drivers and senders interact directly and are responsible for their own arrangements.
  • Drivers are independent participants (not employees, contractors, or agents of the platform).
  • Escrow release: driver marks delivered → sender confirms to release immediately, or it auto-releases after 24 hours.
  • No disputes or refunds after release. The platform does not judge delivery quality or responsibility.
If you don’t have an invite code, ask the pilot organiser for access.
