What is DeliveryMate?

DeliveryMate is a peer-to-peer delivery platform for New Zealand. It's simple: senders post delivery jobs, approved drivers make offers, and payment is held securely in escrow until delivery is confirmed.

We're not a courier company. We're a marketplace that makes it easy for independent drivers and everyday senders to connect, transact safely, and get things moving.
