About DeliveryMate

Driving anyway? That empty boot could help deliver a parcel — and earn you a little extra.

DeliveryMate connects senders with independent drivers for local deliveries in New Zealand. This is an invite‑only pilot for testing and feedback. Email admin@deliverymate.nz with the subject 'Driver Invite Code' or 'Sender Invite Code' to get started.
