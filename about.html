About DeliveryMate

DeliveryMate connects senders with independent drivers for local deliveries in New Zealand. We provide a platform and an escrow workflow, but we do not employ drivers, provide courier services, or mediate disputes. This is an invite‑only pilot for testing and feedback.

Pilot rules

  • Invite‑only test: Stripe is in test mode. Do not use for urgent or high‑value deliveries.
  • Platform only: DeliveryMate connects senders and independent drivers. We are not an employer, courier, or agent.
  • Escrow release: Driver marks delivered → escrow enters pending release. Sender confirms → immediate release. Otherwise auto‑release after 24 hours.
  • No disputes/refunds: No dispute resolution, refunds, or reversals after release.
  • Keep records: Save your Request ID. If you report an issue, include your Request ID and a screenshot.

Contact (pilot)

For pilot access or issues, email admin@deliverymate.nz with your Request ID and a screenshot.
