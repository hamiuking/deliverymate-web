How it works (Driver)

  1. Apply — Admin approval required (usually within 24 hours).
  2. Browse open jobs — Submit offers with your price.
  3. If selected — Pickup item and update status.
  4. Deliver & photo — Upload proof of delivery.
  5. Get paid — Payment releases after delivery confirmation.
