Pilot guide (Driver)

  1. Apply to become a driver (admin approval required).
  2. Browse and submit offers for open requests.
  3. If selected, update status: picked up → delivered.
  4. Payout is initiated after delivery and escrow release (immediate on sender confirmation, or auto‑release after 24 hours).

Drivers are independent. DeliveryMate is not an employer, courier, or agent.
