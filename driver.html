How it works (Driver)

  1. Apply — Admin approval required (usually within 24 hours).
  2. Browse open jobs — Submit offers with your price.
  3. If selected — Pickup item and update status.
  4. Deliver & photo — Upload proof of delivery.
  5. Get paid — Payment releases after delivery confirmation.
⏳ Pending Admin Approval
Your driver profile has been submitted and is awaiting admin review. You cannot submit offers until your account is approved. This usually happens within 24 hours. You will be notified by email once approved.
